Questions at this price point.

How do you market a luxury listing?

With architectural and drone photography, cinematic video and staging guidance, priced on true luxury comparables. We put the listing in front of our luxury and relocation contacts and the out-of-state buyers who make up a growing share of this market, and one of us handles every showing.

Do you work with buyers who live out of state?

Often. We run video tours, coordinate due diligence remotely and manage closings for clients who cannot be here for each step.

What is different about due diligence on an estate?

There is more of everything. Wells and septic systems sized for the home, permits for pools and outbuildings, dock permits on the lake, and the condition of barns and pastures on equestrian property all need a close look.

Can you keep a sale private?

Yes. Some sellers prefer discreet marketing to a known circle of buyers and agents before or instead of a public listing. Tell us what you need and we will explain the trade-offs.

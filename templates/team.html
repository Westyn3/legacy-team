Two generations, one file.

The Legacy Team is Karen Hilliard and her daughter, Britney Javens. Both are Realtors with Keller Williams North Atlanta, and both work every sale.

Most teams split clients between agents. We split the map. Whoever knows your streets leads, and the other reads every contract, covers every showing she can't make, and picks up the phone when the first one is at a closing.

Karen Hilliard

Luxury, Lake Lanier, the mountains and land.

Karen has lived on Lake Lanier for more than twelve years, and her practice started there. She now leads our estate, waterfront, mountain and land work, from Milton and Alpharetta to deep-water homes on Lanier and cabins in Blue Ridge.

Before real estate she worked in construction management. She reads a lot the way a builder does, walks new construction at each stage, and checks docks, wells and septic systems before a client commits. She is licensed in Georgia and in Florida, where she also works with buyers and sellers in Destin and along the Emerald Coast.

How the two of us work a sale.

The same four things hold whether you are buying a first townhome in Chamblee or selling an estate on the lake.

  • The local one leads

    Britney leads south of the lake and Karen leads on it and north of it. In Alpharetta, Milton and Cumming we divide by property type.

  • Both of us know your file

    Either of us can answer your question, show the house or handle the counteroffer. You do not wait for one person's calendar.

  • A builder's eye and a renovator's eye

    Karen's construction management background and Britney's renovation experience mean we look hard at the structure, the systems and the land.

  • Family manners

    We treat clients the way we would want our own family treated: told the truth, called back, and never pushed.

Keller Williams North Atlanta.

We are affiliated with Keller Williams Realty North Atlanta, on North Point Parkway in Alpharetta. Keller Williams is the largest real estate franchise in the world by agent count, and its referral network sends relocating and out-of-state buyers to North Georgia.

You get that reach behind your listing, from two agents who answer their own phones. Each Keller Williams office is independently owned and operated.

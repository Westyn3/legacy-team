We have written before about why we put two Realtors on every sale. This time we want to walk through what that looks like when you are the seller, stage by stage, because the benefits show up in specific moments.

## The first walkthrough

When we can, we both come to the first visit. Karen looks at the house the way a builder would: the roof, the systems, the drainage, the lot and anything a buyer's inspector is likely to flag. Britney looks at it the way a renovator would: which updates will pay for themselves before listing, which ones will not, and how the rooms will read in photographs.

You get one list of recommendations from two points of view, and nothing on it is there for show.

## Pricing

We price from true comparables, and at the upper end that takes judgment. A luxury home in [Alpharetta](/alpharetta/) and one on [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier/) attract different buyers and should never be priced against each other. Two of us reviewing the numbers means a second opinion before the price goes public, which is the cheapest time to get one.

## Preparing and marketing

Britney runs our digital marketing and listing presentation. She coordinates the photography, video and staging guidance and makes sure the listing reads well on every screen. Karen puts the home in front of her luxury and relocation contacts. Keller Williams adds a referral network that reaches buyers moving to North Georgia from out of state.

## Showings

One of us handles every showing. With two agents, that is far easier to keep, and it means the buyer's questions get answered by someone who knows the house. If one of us is at a closing, the other is already familiar with your listing and can step in.

## Offers and negotiation

When an offer comes in, we both read it. One of us usually leads the conversation with the buyer's agent while the other checks the terms, the financing and the dates. On a multiple-offer weekend, having two people means no offer sits unread.

## Inspection and appraisal

This is where Karen's construction background matters most. She can tell you which inspection items are real, which are routine and what a fair response looks like. If a repair is needed, we usually know someone on our [trusted vendors](/trusted-vendors/) list who can quote it quickly.

## Closing day

We coordinate with the closing attorney, the lender and the buyer's agent so the last week is quiet. If you are buying your next home at the same time, the same two of us handle that file too, which keeps both timelines in one place.

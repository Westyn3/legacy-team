Some of our sellers want a sale that never appears online. They may be public figures, they may value their privacy, or they may simply not want neighbors touring the house on a Sunday. We are glad to sell quietly. We also think every seller should understand what privacy costs before choosing it.

## What a private sale looks like

In a private sale, we market the home to a known circle instead of the public. That circle includes buyers we already represent, agents we trust who are working with qualified luxury buyers, and relocation contacts through the Keller Williams referral network. We share details and photographs only with people who have a genuine reason to see them, and we arrange showings around your schedule.

## Who it suits

A private sale works best for a few kinds of sellers. Those who have time and are not under pressure to close by a date. Those with a distinctive property that a small number of serious buyers will recognize. And those for whom privacy is worth more than the last dollar of price.

It can also be a useful first stage. Some sellers start quietly for a few weeks, then move to a full public launch if the right buyer has not appeared.

## The trade-offs

The main cost is exposure. A home seen by fewer buyers will usually receive fewer offers, and competition is what pushes a price upward. A private sale gives up some of that leverage. There may also be rules from the local listing service about how and when a home can be marketed before it is listed publicly, and we will explain how those apply to your situation before we begin.

The other cost is time. Finding the one buyer can take longer when you are not advertising.

## How we protect your privacy either way

Even in a fully public sale, we can limit what is shared. We can leave interior photographs of private rooms out of the listing, require pre-approval or proof of funds before a showing, and make sure one of us attends every visit. For our [luxury homes](/luxury-homes/), discretion is the default.

## Pricing still comes first

A private sale only works if the price is right from the first conversation. With fewer buyers seeing the home, there is less market feedback to correct a price that is too high. We price from true luxury comparables and review the number together before we share it with anyone.

## Two of us, fewer people involved

With a team of two, your information stays with two people. Both of us know your file, so a buyer's agent always reaches someone who can answer, and nobody else needs to be involved.

A lot of my clients come to me with the same short list: Buckhead, Brookhaven and Sandy Springs. All three sit close to the center of Atlanta, all three have beautiful homes, and they feel very different once you live in them. Here is how I help people choose.

## Buckhead, if you want the classic address

[Buckhead](/buckhead/) covers the widest range of any part of Atlanta. The estate streets of Tuxedo Park and West Paces Ferry are the top of the city's market. The towers along Peachtree Road suit owners who want to lock the door and travel. Garden Hills and Peachtree Heights offer sidewalk neighborhoods of classic homes, and the streets around Chastain Park put the city's largest park at your door.

The trade is traffic and price. Most of my Buckhead clients decide it is worth it, because nothing else in Atlanta offers the same restaurants, shops and history within a few minutes of home.

## Brookhaven, if you want to walk to dinner

[Brookhaven](/brookhaven/) suits buyers who want neighborhood life. Around the Village and Dresden Drive you can walk to restaurants and the MARTA station, and the streets around them have sidewalks and parks. Historic Brookhaven, set around the Capital City Club, is one of the city's most lasting estate addresses.

Brookhaven is also where my renovation background earns its keep. A 1950s ranch can sit beside a new craftsman priced far above it, and either can be the right purchase at the right number. I help clients tell which is which.

## Sandy Springs, if you want trees and space

[Sandy Springs](/sandy-springs/) sits between the Chattahoochee River and the Perimeter, about ten minutes from Buckhead. Established neighborhoods have large wooded lots, the riverside areas feel like a retreat, and City Springs has given the city a walkable center with a performing arts center and a green. It is a strong choice for buyers who want room without moving far from the city.

## Questions that settle it

When a client is torn, I ask a few things. Where do you drive every weekday morning? Do you want to walk anywhere, or do you want privacy? Is a yard important, or would you rather have a view and a concierge? Which schools matter to you? School zones can change from one block to the next in all three cities, so I confirm them for every address.

Then we spend a Saturday in each one. Coffee in Brookhaven Village, lunch in Buckhead, a walk by the river in Sandy Springs. By the evening most people know.

Clients tend to remember two things about working with me: that I answer quickly, and that I follow through. Neither happens by accident. Here is how my mom and I keep a search moving in North Atlanta, where the homes people want most often do not wait.

## We set up the search properly

A good search starts with a clear brief. Before we tour anything, I ask about commutes, schools, the size of the yard you want, how much renovation you are willing to take on and the neighborhoods you already like. Then I set up alerts that are specific enough to be useful. A search that sends you forty homes a day is a search you stop reading.

## You hear from a person

When a home matches your brief, you hear from me, usually with a note about what the photos leave out. I tell you whether I think it is worth seeing and why. If the kitchen will need a full redo or a wall can easily come out, I say so. My renovation background means I can usually give you a rough sense of the cost.

## Two calendars instead of one

The biggest reason we move quickly is simple: there are two of us. If I am at a closing in [Roswell](/roswell/) when a home comes up in [Johns Creek](/johns-creek/), my mom, Karen, can show it that afternoon. She already knows your file and what you are looking for, so you lose nothing by seeing it with her.

## Ready before the offer

We get the slow parts done early. That means a pre-approval letter in hand, proof of funds organized, and a clear idea of the terms you care about. When the right house appears, we can write a strong offer the same day without rushing the decisions that matter.

## Follow-through after the offer

Speed matters after acceptance too. Inspections, appraisal, financing and closing each have deadlines, and missing one can cost you leverage or the house itself. I track every date and send reminders before they arrive. Karen reviews inspection reports with the eye of someone who worked in construction management, so we can respond to the seller quickly and with confidence.

## Quick without pressure

Moving quickly never means pressuring a client. If a home is not right, I will tell you, and I will be glad you passed. Speed is there so that when you are ready to decide, nothing stands in your way.

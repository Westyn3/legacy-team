Out-of-state buyers make up a growing share of the top of the lake market, and most of them begin their search online. Few can spend every weekend in Georgia while they decide. We have built our process so that most of the work happens before a client boards a plane, and the trip itself is spent on a short list we already trust.

## Start with how you will use the water

Before we send a single listing, Karen asks how you plan to use the lake. A boater who wants deep water and a quiet cove should look at a different shore than a family that needs Buford City Schools or a short drive to Atlanta. That one conversation narrows close to 700 miles of shoreline to a few areas. Our [Lake Lanier guide](/lake-lanier/) lays out how the sides of the lake compare.

## Video tours that show what photos leave out

Listing photos are taken when a property looks its best. On a live video tour, we show the things you would check in person: the walk from the house to the dock, the slope of the yard, the view across the cove and the condition of the mechanical systems. You direct the camera, and we stay on the call until your questions run out.

## What we verify before you fly in

Remote due diligence on a lake home reaches well past the house. Before a client travels, we confirm:

- the Corps of Engineers dock permit and the dock size it allows
- how much water the dock holds when the lake is full and when it drops in the cooler months
- school assignment, which can change block to block near the Gainesville city line and does not always follow the map in Buford
- well and septic records, where the home sits outside sewer service
- the drive times that matter to you, such as Alpharetta or downtown Atlanta

These checks remove properties that look right in photos and fail on the details. For estate-level purchases, our [luxury homes](/luxury-homes/) page describes the added due diligence.

Karen leads on the lake. She has called Lanier home for more than twelve years and brings her construction-management background to the dock, the slope, the systems and any planned build. Britney's background is in flipping and renovating homes, so she looks closely at finishes, condition and what a house will need after closing. Britney also leads in North Atlanta and intown, which helps when a client wants a city address alongside the lake home or is selling one to buy the other. We both work every sale, so either of us can pick up your call. You can read more about each of us on the [team page](/team/).

## The trip itself

When you come to Georgia, the visit is for decisions. We plan the day around a short list that has already passed our checks, so the hours go to comparing finalists. After that, we coordinate the remaining steps and the closing for clients who cannot be here for each one.

Some buyers want a dock of their own and as little company as possible. Others want a clubhouse, a marina and neighbors who use the water the same way they do. For the second group, [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier/) has a handful of communities built around a club or a marina, and each has its own character. These are the ones clients ask me about most, and the kind of owner I think each one fits.

## Harbour Point and Marina Bay

Both are gated communities on the Gainesville shore. Harbour Point offers the fullest amenity list on the lake: a yacht club and marina, along with a pool and tennis courts. Marina Bay lies close to Lake Lanier Olympic Park. Its resort-style marina and clubhouse serve a mix of lakefront and lake-view homes. Gated deep-water estates in both run well into seven figures.

Who they suit: the full-time lake owner who wants a marina and the social life of a club, with downtown [Gainesville](/gainesville/) a short drive away.

## Chattahoochee Country Club

This is Gainesville's private club for golf and the lake. Its fairways follow the shoreline, and the homes on the course and the water rarely change hands. When one does, it often goes to a buyer who has been waiting for it.

Who it suits: the golfer first, and anyone who wants an established club address with lake access. Patience is part of buying here. If this is the address you want, tell me early so we are ready when a home appears.

## Cresswind at Lake Lanier

Cresswind is an active-adult community for buyers 55 and older, with a clubhouse on the lake and a marina. It draws active retirees from around the country, and its resale market ranks with the strongest for 55+ buyers in North Georgia.

Who it suits: retirees and those close to it who want the lake, a full social calendar and the ease of a single community with shared amenities.

## Chestatee

On the northern shore in Dawson County, Chestatee pairs golf with the lake, along with its own marina and clubhouse. Homes range from golf-course lots to deep-water lakefront, with prices that start in the $500s and reach seven figures. The north end is quieter and deeper than the south, and that calm sets the tone here.

Who it suits: the buyer who wants club amenities and deep water away from the busier south end. My [Dawsonville](/dawsonville/) page covers the surrounding area.

## Sterling on the Lake and the Aqualand Marina area

In [Flowery Branch](/flowery-branch/), Sterling on the Lake is a resort-style master-planned community with several pools, a fitness center, clubhouses, lakes, trails and a village center. Homes run from the $400s into the $800s. Nearby, the Aqualand Marina area sits beside one of the largest marinas on Lanier. For a buyer who wants a boat slip without a lakefront price, I count this corner of the lake among the better values on the water.

Who it suits: families who want community amenities, a workable commute on I-985 and the lake close at hand.

We are Karen Hilliard and Britney Javens, a mother and her daughter, and we sell real estate together with Keller Williams North Atlanta. People ask how that works more often than they ask about anything else, so we want to answer it properly at the start of a new year.

## How the team came together

Karen started her practice on [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier/), where she has lived for more than twelve years. Before real estate she worked in construction management, and that shaped how she looks at land, new builds and the systems inside a home. Britney came into the business through flipping and renovating houses, and she built her practice from Buckhead up the GA-400 corridor.

For a while we each ran our own business and sent clients to each other. A family moving from [Johns Creek](/johns-creek/) to the lake would start with one of us and finish with the other. Eventually it made more sense to stop handing clients off and start working every file together. That is The Legacy Team.

## What a family team changes for you

Most people assume a team means more people and less attention. Ours is the reverse. There are only two of us, and we both work every sale, so you get two experienced agents who know your file in detail.

We also talk to each other constantly, the way a mother and daughter do. A question you raise with Britney on Tuesday morning is something Karen knows about by Tuesday afternoon. Nobody has to send a memo or wait for a weekly meeting.

## We divide the map, not the client

Britney leads in [Buckhead](/buckhead/), Brookhaven, Sandy Springs, Roswell and Johns Creek. Karen leads on the lake and north into the mountains, from [Gainesville](/gainesville/) to Blue Ridge. In Alpharetta, Milton and Cumming, where we both work, we split by property: Britney takes the neighborhoods, townhomes and new construction, and Karen takes the estates, gated communities and acreage.

Whoever knows your streets leads. The other reads every contract, covers showings and answers the phone when the first one is at a closing.

## Two kinds of eyes on every house

Karen reads a property the way a builder does: the lot, the structure, the well, the septic, the dock. Britney reads it the way a renovator does: what the kitchen will cost to redo, which wall can come out, which update is worth paying for. On a showing we often notice different things, and our clients hear both views before they decide.

## Why we call it a legacy

The name is partly about us, a family business passed from one generation to the next. It is also about our clients. A home is often the largest thing a family owns and the thing it passes down. We take that seriously, and we treat each client the way we would want our own family treated: told the truth, called back and never pushed.

White-glove is a phrase every luxury agent uses, and it rarely comes with a definition. I would rather tell you what it means in practice on our team, step by step, so you can hold us to it.

## It starts with listening

Before we look at a single listing, Britney or I will sit down with you and ask how you want to live. How you use the weekends. Who visits and how long they stay. Whether you want a boat, a barn, a view or a doorman. A seven-figure home in [Milton](/milton/) and a seven-figure home on the lake serve very different lives, and I do not want you touring houses that were never right for you.

## We do the screening before you arrive

At this price point your time is the scarcest thing in the search. We preview homes ahead of you whenever we can, and we tell you plainly which ones are not worth the drive. When a home is worth seeing, we arrive knowing the comparables, the questions to ask and what the listing photos leave out.

For clients who live out of state, we run video tours and walk the property the way you would, opening closets, standing on the dock and stepping off the deck to look at the roofline.

## Due diligence sized to the property

An estate has more of everything: wells and septic systems sized for the home, permits for pools and outbuildings, barns and pastures on equestrian land and dock permits on [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier/). Before I worked in real estate I worked in construction management, so I look at these the way a builder would. When something needs a specialist, we bring in one we trust, and we are there when they are.

## Two agents on your file

On our team, both of us work every purchase. If I am on the water with another client when an inspection report comes in, Britney already knows your file and can walk you through it. You do not wait for one person's calendar, and you never get passed to an assistant.

## Discretion as a default

Many of our luxury clients prefer privacy. We do not post about your search, we do not share your name with listing agents until it helps you, and we keep your financial details with the people who need them. If you want a purchase handled quietly from start to finish, tell us at the start.

## The closing, and the week after

We coordinate the lender, the closing attorney, the inspectors and anyone else involved so you are not chasing signatures. After closing, we stay available. Clients call us for a landscaper, a dock builder or a painter, and our [trusted vendors](/trusted-vendors/) list exists because those calls come in so often.

That is what white-glove service means to us: fewer wasted hours, a closer look at the property and two people who answer their phones. If you are planning a purchase, see our [luxury homes](/luxury-homes/) page or [get in touch](/contact/) and we will start with that first conversation.

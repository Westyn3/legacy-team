A seven-figure home is usually seen online long before anyone walks through the door. Many of the buyers at this level live out of state, and they decide whether to visit from what the listing shows them. So my work on a luxury sale starts well before the sign goes up, and it follows the same order every time.

## Step one: price against the right homes

The valuation is a comparative market analysis, and at this price the comparables decide almost everything. A Milton estate and a deep-water home on Lake Lanier can sit at similar prices. They still draw different buyers and carry different risks. I price lakefront against lakefront and estates against estates. A county average tells a luxury seller very little. Getting the number right at the start is the most valuable thing I do for a client.

## Step two: prepare and stage

Next we tour the house room by room, and I put together a written plan. It lists what should go into storage, what should be staged, and which repairs are worth their cost at sale. I can refer the vendors for that work. Staging guidance is specific: what to keep, what to move and what to bring in before the photographer arrives. My years in construction management help here. I can tell a seller which items a buyer's inspector is likely to flag and which ones are only cosmetic.

## Step three: photography and film

Every listing gets professional architectural photography and drone aerials. Aerials carry the most weight on estate lots, golf-course homes and waterfront, where the setting is much of the value. On the lake I add photography from water level. Then comes cinematic video, a film of the house and grounds, and on Lake Lanier the dock and what the property looks like from a boat. For a buyer who lives in another state, this film is the first showing.

## Step four: reach the right buyers, privately if you prefer

The listing goes to Georgia MLS and FMLS and is syndicated to Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com and other real estate sites. That is the baseline. At this level, the reach that counts comes through our luxury and relocation contacts and the Keller Williams referral network, which is how a listing finds people moving to North Georgia from elsewhere. For those buyers I run video tours and help coordinate due diligence and closing remotely.

One of us attends every showing, so a buyer's questions about the well, the dock permit or the mechanical systems get a real answer on the spot. My daughter Britney shares every listing with me, which means someone who knows the file is always available.

Some sellers would prefer that their home never appears on a public portal. For them we offer discreet marketing to a known circle of buyers and agents, either before a public listing or in place of one. It protects privacy, and it narrows the audience. I explain both sides plainly and let the seller decide.

## Step five: negotiate and close

When offers arrive, I weigh each one on price, contingencies and closing timeline, and I advise on the strongest overall. From there we manage inspections, repair requests, the appraisal and the paperwork through closing day.

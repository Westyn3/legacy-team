Families rarely make just one move. A couple buys a first home intown, moves up to the suburbs when the children arrive, adds a lake house, and years later sells the family home to live somewhere simpler. Their grown children start the cycle again. As a mother and daughter, we understand that rhythm personally, and we built our team to follow it.

## The first home

Many of our clients' first purchases are townhomes and condos in places like [Chamblee](/chamblee/), Brookhaven and Sandy Springs, close to work and restaurants. Britney leads most of these searches. First-time buyers need patience and plain explanations, and she gives both.

## The family home

When the family grows, the search usually moves up the GA-400 corridor toward schools and space in [Johns Creek](/johns-creek/), Alpharetta, Milton or Cumming. This is where our split by property type helps. Britney knows the neighborhoods and new construction. Karen knows the estates, gated communities and acreage. The family works with whoever fits the house they want, and both of us stay on the file.

## The lake or mountain place

Later, many families add a second home on [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier/) or in the mountains. Karen leads that work. She has lived on the lake for more than twelve years and knows the coves, the docks and the communities. Because she already knows the family from the earlier purchase, the search starts with real understanding.

## The move to something smaller

Eventually the family home becomes more house than anyone needs. That sale is often emotional, and it usually comes with a purchase: a smaller home near the grandchildren, a lock-and-leave condo in Buckhead or a full-time move to the lake. We coordinate both sides so the timelines work, and we treat the house with the respect it has earned. Our [sell](/sell/) page covers how we prepare a long-held home for market.

## The next generation

The children who grew up in that house will one day buy their own. When they call us, we already know their family, and they already know they will be treated well. That continuity is what we mean by The Legacy Team.

## Why two generations help

A mother and daughter see things differently. Karen brings a construction background and years on the lake to questions of land and long-term value. Britney brings a renovator's eye and a strong sense of what younger buyers are looking for. Families with members at different stages of life often find that each of them relates to one of us, and the two of us talk about every file.

## Family manners

Whatever the move, we hold to the same standard: tell clients the truth, call them back and never push. We would want nothing less for our own family.

If someone in your family is planning a move, any kind of move, [get in touch](/contact/) or meet us on [the team page](/team/). We would be glad to help with this one and the ones that follow.

People sometimes assume a team means you meet one agent and then get passed to an assistant. Ours works the other way. There are two of us, Karen and me, and we both work every sale.

## We split the map

I lead from [Buckhead](/buckhead/) up the GA-400 corridor to [Cumming](/cumming/). My mom leads on [Lake Lanier](/lake-lanier/) and north into the mountains. In [Alpharetta](/alpharetta/) and [Milton](/milton/), where we both work, we divide by property: I take the neighborhoods, townhomes and new construction, and she takes estates, gated communities and acreage.

## The other one always knows your file

When a home you would love comes up and I am at a closing, Karen can show it. When an offer needs an answer and she is on the lake with another client, I have the paperwork. In a fast market that matters.

## Two kinds of experience

I came into this business flipping and renovating homes, so I notice what a house needs and what it will cost. Karen worked in construction management and reads land, new builds and big systems the way a contractor does. Between us, most surprises get caught before closing.

## You can move without starting over

Clients change direction. A search that begins with a family home in [Johns Creek](/johns-creek/) turns into a lake house. A seller in Milton decides the next place is a cabin in [Blue Ridge](/blue-ridge/). With us you keep the same two people, and nobody has to learn your story again.

If that sounds like the way you would want to be looked after, [get in touch](/contact/). Either of us will answer.

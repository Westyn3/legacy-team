Brookhaven earned its standing through streets that people enjoy walking. At the center, beside the MARTA station, Dresden Drive and Brookhaven Village supply a true restaurant row and places to gather. Around them, Ashford Park, Lynwood Park and Brookhaven Fields offer renovated bungalows and new craftsman homes, along with a front-porch way of life that intown buyers pay extra to find. Britney leads our Brookhaven work and spends much of her time on these blocks.

The upper end of the market sits to the west. Historic Brookhaven surrounds the Capital City Club's golf course and contains some of the most elegant estate homes in Atlanta. Elsewhere, Oglethorpe University brings a gothic campus, Murphey Candler Park adds a lake and ball fields, and the young city has invested heavily in its parks. Buyers at this level are often choosing between an estate here and one in Buckhead, and we show both. Taken together, these assets give Brookhaven more presence than its size would suggest.

Demand has reshaped the housing stock. Original ranches from the 1950s now stand beside new craftsman homes priced above $1.5M, sometimes on adjoining lots, and that mix makes values hard to read from the curb. We lean on Britney's experience renovating homes to judge condition and to price accurately, and we treat inspections as essential. For sellers, the same knowledge shapes how we position a property against the new construction down the street.

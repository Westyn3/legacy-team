Blairsville is the county seat of Union County, on Georgia's northern border, and it was founded near the Nottely River. In 1942 the Tennessee Valley Authority dammed that river to form Lake Nottely, which lies entirely within the county. Brasstown Bald, the highest mountain in Georgia, rises on the county line. To the south, Vogel State Park, one of the state's oldest, sits at the base of Blood Mountain, and the Appalachian Trail crosses the county. The Chattahoochee National Forest covers nearly two-thirds of the land.

That much public land shapes the market. Private land here can border national forest, which helps protect views and privacy. Buyers come for lake homes on Nottely, cabins along the river, and acreage with long-range views toward the high peaks. Many are buying a second home they plan to use more each year, and some intend to move here full-time. The town keeps its own calendar too, including the Sorghum Festival each October.

Forsyth County has grown substantially in people and housing for more than a decade, and Cumming sits at the middle of it. Buyers come for the school system, for the range of new construction, and because a budget covers more house and land here than in North Fulton. GA-400 keeps Alpharetta and its technology employers a short drive south. The growth now has a center in the Cumming City Center, alongside The Collection at Forsyth, the Big Creek Greenway, and Sawnee Mountain with its trails and Indian Seats overlook.

The lake is what separates Cumming from the rest of the corridor. Forsyth County holds the southwest corner of Lake Lanier, and its parks, marinas and coves put boating within reach of a normal evening. Housing spans the full range. Vickery offers village streets and walkable shops. Master-planned communities supply new homes, and older swim-tennis neighborhoods provide larger lots. At the top, estate homes off Highway 9 and deep-water lakefront carry prices well into seven figures.

We cover Cumming as a pair. Britney works with families in the swim-tennis neighborhoods, townhomes and new communities, often showing clients how far an Alpharetta budget travels one county north. Karen leads on lakefront, gated and golf communities and large-lot estates, and she has lived on Lanier for more than twelve years. With so much of the inventory new or recently built, her construction background is especially useful. She reviews builder contracts and walks new builds for our clients, whichever of us began the search.

Buckhead is where Atlanta keeps its landmarks. The mansions of Tuxedo Park and West Paces Ferry, the Atlanta History Center and the shops of Buckhead Village all sit within it, along with a restaurant scene the rest of the city measures itself against. Yet the area is broader than its estate reputation. Peachtree Road carries some of the best luxury high-rise and condo living in the Southeast, and Garden Hills, Peachtree Heights and Peachtree Hills offer walkable streets lined with classic Atlanta houses.

Chastain Park anchors the northern edge. It is the city's largest park, home to an amphitheater concert series, a golf course, a horse park and a tennis center, and the houses around it are some of the best-loved addresses intown. Families find a rare pairing here: the energy of the city alongside top private schools, solid public options and green space outside the front door. Britney leads our Buckhead work and knows these streets closely.

Pricing runs from condos in the $300s to penthouses, through renovated bungalows and classic homes in the $1M to $3M core, and on to estates far above.

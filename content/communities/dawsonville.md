Dawsonville links the northern suburbs of metro Atlanta to the North Georgia mountains. GA-400 leads directly to it and ends close to the North Georgia Premium Outlets, leaving Alpharetta within 45 minutes. That access has made Dawson County one of Georgia's fastest-growing rural counties. Buyers arrive looking for land, views and lake access, at prices Forsyth County passed years ago. Karen leads our work here, and she brings clients who want lake or mountains while staying under an hour from Alpharetta.

The lake has a different character at this end. Lanier's northern reaches are deeper, quieter and less developed than its south end. Chestatee sets a golf course beside lake access, one of the more distinctive settings on the water. Further north is Big Canoe, a gated mountain community with lakes, golf and fully amenitized mountain homes. For time outdoors, residents have Amicalola Falls and the Dawson Forest Wildlife Management Area close by.

The market takes in lakefront estates, mountain homes, new-construction neighborhoods beside GA-400, acreage and buildable lots. Karen's background in construction and land is most useful in a county like this one. Buyers here often study well and septic, topography, road access and buildability before they tour a finished house. For clients selling in North Atlanta and buying acreage or lakefront here, Britney and Karen handle the two ends of the move as one team.

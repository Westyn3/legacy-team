Few lake communities can offer a school district of their own. Buford can, and that single fact shapes its market. Buford City Schools ranks consistently among Georgia's top-performing districts, so homes zoned for it cost more and tend to keep their value. The town itself lies across the Gwinnett and Hall county line at the southern tip of Lake Lanier. Karen leads our work here, and her first step with any buyer is settling the zoning question, city or county, before a neighborhood is chosen.

The water is close. Lake Lanier Islands, with a resort, water park, marina and beaches, is within reach, and Holiday Marina and the Buford Dam area mark the lake's south end. Away from the shore, the old downtown has been revitalized, and its Main Street now draws people for restaurants and shops. Everyday retail and dining run along the Mall of Georgia corridor beside I-985, which also connects the town to northern Gwinnett's job base.

Housing covers a wide span: historic homes near downtown, large swim-tennis communities, newer construction along Hamilton Mill and Highway 20, and lakefront on the south shore. For our clients at the upper end, that south shore is where we spend the most time, and Karen's years on the lake inform every showing. Because Britney covers North Atlanta and intown, a family weighing Buford against Duluth or Johns Creek can compare both with one team.

# Journal post spec (The Legacy Team site)

One file per post: content/journal/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md (slug: lowercase words joined by hyphens, 3-7 words).

```
---
title: "..."            # 45-70 characters, sentence case, no colon-then-reveal, no clickbait
date: YYYY-MM-DD        # a Friday, exactly as assigned
author: karen           # karen | britney | team
description: "..."      # 120-155 characters, plain statement of what the reader learns
---
Body in markdown: 500-700 words. An opening paragraph (no heading), then 3-5 sections each starting with "## Heading"
(sentence case). End with one short paragraph that invites the reader to get in touch, linking /contact/.
```

## Voice
- author: karen -> first person singular as Karen Hilliard ("I"). Refer to Britney as "my daughter Britney" or "Britney" at most once.
- author: britney -> first person singular as Britney Javens. Refer to Karen as "my mom, Karen" or "Karen".
- author: team -> "we" (Karen and Britney together).
- Luxury through restraint: specific, calm, expert. Plain verbs, short sentences.
- Banned: "stunning", "dream home", "nestled", "boasts", "unparalleled", "breathtaking", "oasis", "hidden gem",
  "look no further", "in today's market", "whether you're", exclamation marks, ALL CAPS, rhetorical-question openers,
  em-dash asides, "not X, but Y" constructions.

## Facts
Every factual claim (prices, names of communities/clubs/marinas, regulations, distances, history, school names,
acreage) must come from the source files you are given. Do NOT add facts from general knowledge. No market statistics,
no interest rates, no "inventory is down X%", no claims about current market conditions. Opinions, process, and
advice are fine. Do not invent clients, sales, testimonials, or anecdotes about specific deals.

## Originality
Karen's and Britney's own sites already publish posts on some of these topics. Do not reuse their sentences, structure
or titles. Read the listed existing posts and write a clearly different piece.
